# Customer Service Scenario 4: Prioritizing Requests

## Situation

Three customers contacted support at the same time: one had a general question, one had a delayed order, and one had an issue that needed immediate attention.

## How I Would Handle It

I would categorize each request based on urgency. The general question would be Normal, the delayed order Urgent, and the issue needing immediate attention Very Urgent and escalated when necessary.

## Skills Demonstrated

Prioritization • Communication • Problem-solving • Escalation
