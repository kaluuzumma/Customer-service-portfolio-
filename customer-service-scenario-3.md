# Customer Service Scenario 3: Escalated Complaint

## Situation

A customer was very upset about an unresolved issue and asked to speak with a manager.

## How I Would Handle It

I would stay calm, listen to the customer, and try to understand the issue. If I could not resolve it within my role, I would escalate it to the appropriate team or manager and explain the next steps to the customer.

## Skills Demonstrated

Communication • Empathy • Escalation • Problem-solving
