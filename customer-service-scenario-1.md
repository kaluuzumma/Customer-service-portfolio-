# Customer Service Scenario 1: Delayed Order

## Situation

A customer contacted me because their order was late. They were frustrated because they needed the item urgently.

## How I Would Handle It

I would apologize for the delay and let the customer know I understand why they are frustrated. I would check the order and tracking information to find out what happened.

I would then explain the latest update in a clear and simple way. If there was a problem with the delivery, I would explain the options available and help the customer decide what to do next.

## Goal

My goal would be to solve the issue while making sure the customer feels listened to and respected.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Communication

- Empathy

- Problem-solving

- Active listening

- Customer service
